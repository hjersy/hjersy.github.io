TL;DR

2–3 sentences for a busy hiring manager: the problem, what you did, the result. Many readers stop here, so make it count.

01 · ProblemWhat was broken, and for whom

Describe the users, their workflow and the pain. Use evidence: quotes, numbers, support data. Explain why this was worth solving for the business.

02 · ConstraintsWhy this was hard

  • Enterprise constraints: permissions, compliance, audit, data privacy
  • AI constraints: hallucination risk, latency, cost, model limits
  • Organizational constraints: timeline, legacy systems, stakeholders

03 · ProcessHow I approached it

Show your AI-native process: how you used AI to speed up research synthesis, how you prototyped against a real model, and how you tested real outputs with users.

04 · Key decisionsThe calls that shaped the product

Decision 1: Suggest, don't auto-apply

Options considered, the tradeoff, why you chose this, and what users said.

Decision 2: Show sources and confidence inline

How you designed for trust and made AI output easy to check.

Decision 3: Design the failure states first

What happens when the model is wrong, slow or unsure, and how users recover.
