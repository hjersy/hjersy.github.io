Approach

How I work as an AI-native designer

01

Designing for AI, not around it

AI products only work when people can predict, check and correct them. I design for trust first: clear sources, confidence, graceful failure and human control.

  • Human-in-the-loop review
  • Citations & explainability
  • Error & uncertainty states
02

Prototyping in real code

Static mockups can't show how an AI feature behaves. I build working prototypes against real models, so teams test real outputs instead of best-case screenshots.

  • Code prototypes with live LLMs
  • Prompt & output evaluation
  • Close collaboration with engineering
03

AI-accelerated research

I use AI to synthesize interviews, tickets and usage data faster. The time saved goes into talking to more users, not less.

  • Rapid synthesis & affinity mapping
  • Support-ticket & log mining
  • Faster, more frequent iteration
04

Enterprise-grade rigor

Speed is useless if it skips the hard parts. Roles and permissions, scale, compliance, accessibility and long-term consistency are built in from day one.

  • Complex roles & permissions
  • WCAG accessibility
  • Systems that scale across teams
